## Section B — Case Analyses (10 pts each, 30 pts)

### Case 1 — The Cosmetics Test
A Czech cosmetics start-up wants to bring a new sunscreen to global markets. The EU has banned animal testing for cosmetics (Regulation 1223/2009), but the company plans to sell in a country that *requires* animal testing for import. The R&D director proposes a compromise: run a minimum number of rabbit eye-irritation tests (Draize test) in a non-EU lab while pursuing validated *in vitro* alternatives in parallel.

a) Identify which **3R** principle each part of the proposal does or does not respect. (3 pts)
b) Construct a **utilitarian** and a **Kantian** evaluation of the plan. (4 pts)
c) From a **virtue-ethics** standpoint, what would a person of good character (a *phronimos*) do here? (3 pts)

### Case 2 — Open Data, Vulnerable Populations
A genomics consortium wants to publish whole-genome sequences from a small Indigenous community in an open repository under a CC0 license to maximize FAIR compliance. The community elders have agreed to participate but want a say over future secondary uses (e.g., commercial products, ancestry research).

a) Is full CC0 release compatible with the **CARE** principles? Explain. (3 pts)
b) Propose a data-sharing model that is FAIR-compliant *and* respects CARE. (4 pts)
c) Which of Ross-Hellauer's "cumulative advantage" risks does this case illustrate? (3 pts)

### Case 3 — The Pandemic Trolley
A novel pathogen is spreading. A vaccine candidate works in mice but the predictive value of the mouse model is uncertain. A regulator must decide: (i) skip Phase I human trials and deploy widely; (ii) run accelerated Phase I + small Phase II in volunteers; (iii) delay 18 months for full standard trials. 100,000 people will die per month of delay.

a) Give the strongest **utilitarian** argument for option (i) and the strongest **Kantian** objection to it. (4 pts)
b) How does the **Helsinki Declaration** (mentioned in the Soucitně article) constrain the calculation? (3 pts)
c) Where does **descriptive ethics** (Haidt, Greene) help us understand likely public reactions to each option? (3 pts)

## Section C — Compare and Contrast (20 pts)

Write a structured essay (≈1–1.5 pages) addressing **both** of the following:

**Part 1 (10 pts).** Compare Singer (sentience-utilitarianism) and Regan (rights-deontology) as **theoretical foundations** for animal-ethics regulation. Which gives a better account of the 3R framework currently in EU law, and why?

**Part 2 (10 pts).** "Sandel's Aristotelian framework offers something neither Bentham nor Kant can: an account of *why we honor* certain practices." Defend or challenge this claim using at least one bioethical example (e.g., medicine, parenthood, sport, or research integrity).
